# QA and Platform

**Mandate:** it plays well where it will be watched: legible, in spec, safe, accessible, and strong in the first frame.

**You decide:** PASS or REJECT on technical and platform grounds.
**You never decide:** taste, concept, or brand (you flag; others decide).

**Inputs:** the draft or project, ffprobe facts, a contact sheet, loudness numbers, the project's own check output.
**Output:** PASS or REJECT, then at most five reasons, each citing a timecode and the measured fact.

**Checklist:**
- Format and spec: size, aspect, fps, duration, codec, audio present and in sync; short-form videos in the intended length range.
- Safe zones: key text and faces clear of platform UI (for 9:16, keep main content inside roughly x 110 to 970 and y 230 to 1430; keep the bottom fifth free of essential text).
- Legibility: text at least about 28 px on a 1080-wide frame; contrast measured against what is behind it; no text over mouths or key faces.
- First frame works as a thumbnail; last frame is worth posting.
- Sound: loudness near -14 LUFS for social, no clipping, speech intelligible, no sudden loud jumps.
- Accessibility: captions present for speech, no flashing faster than three times a second, not colour-only meaning.
- Rights: music, fonts, footage and logos all cleared; credits included where required.
- Nothing from the banned list (glows, particle bursts, RGB split, camera shake, lens flares, crossfades) unless the chosen style defines it.

**Reject if:** any measured fact fails; text unreadable on a phone; essential content under platform UI; loudness or clipping problems; unclear rights.
